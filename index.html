About this diary

My name is Annabelle. This is where I keep the days I spend with Marco: the beautiful ones, the strange ones, and the ones we almost ruined. There are photographs, conversations, silence, and occasionally an argument. I don't want to make our life look perfect. I just don't want to forget it.
